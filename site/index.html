How it works

Your to-do list and your pet share one loop.

📝

Pick a task

Add up to 7 to-dos on the web page, then choose one on the device.

⏳

Focus

25 or 50 minutes with an animated hourglass and a soft heartbeat sound.

🥕

Earn

Carrots, Growth and Stars. 25 min = 1 carrot, 50 min = 2.

🐰

Care

Feed, pet and play. Your rabbit grows: Egg, Baby, Kid, Teen, Adult.

Meet the rabbit

Pixel art drawn 1-bit on a 128×160 screen. It hatches, plays, gets moody and sleeps at night.

⭐ Rewards shop

Earn Stars (25 min = 1, 50 min = 2, finished task +1, max 8 a day) and dress your rabbit from the web page.

🎀 Bow 5🌸 Flower 8🥳 Party hat 12 🧢 Cap 15🎩 Top hat 25👑 Crown 40 + 30-day streak 🌱 Grass 10🌧️ Rain 15✨ Stars 25🪔 Festival lamps 40 + 100-day streak

Features

Everything runs on the ESP8266 itself. No cloud, no app required.

⏱️Pomodoro presets25 → 5 break, 50 → 10 break, every 4th gets a 15 min long break. Quick 10 min focus too.
🔔Soothing soundsStart bell, 264 Hz heartbeat loop during focus, and a gentle done chime. Hold MODE to mute.
✅To-do linked focusFocus on a task, then answer "Task done?". Unfinished tasks carry over to tomorrow.
🔥Streaks & shieldsDaily streak, best streak, a shield every 7 days, milestones at 3, 7, 30 and 100.
🌐Built-in web pageOpen pomodoro.local for to-dos, stats, history, shop, settings and backups.
📊Weekly reportSun–Sat focus bars, best day, best task and care counts, built in your browser.
🕗Knows the timeGood morning at 8, lunch at 1, yawns at 10 pm, sleeps at night. NTP time with an offline fallback.
📱Phone alertsFree ntfy notifications: break over, hungry rabbit, evening summary.
🔋Power-cut safeTwo checksummed save slots, and a running focus resumes after a reset or power cut.
🪔Festival dayMark a holiday and the rabbit skips all penalties that day.
📶Easy Wi-Fi setupA Pomodoro-Setup hotspot with a captive portal. No passwords in code.
😴Idle sleepAfter an hour idle the backlight dims and Wi-Fi switches off. Any button wakes it.

Flash it

Arduino IDE, about 10 minutes.

  1. Install the Arduino IDE and add the ESP8266 boards URL: https://arduino.esp8266.com/stable/package_esp8266com_index.json
  2. Boards Manager → install esp8266 by ESP8266 Community (tested with 3.1.2).
  3. Library Manager → install Adafruit GFX and Adafruit ST7735 and ST7789.
  4. Open pomodoro_rabbit_v2/pomodoro_rabbit_v2.ino. Board: NodeMCU 1.0 (ESP-12E), Flash: 4MB (FS:2MB), Erase: Only Sketch.
  5. Upload. Join the Pomodoro-Setup hotspot from your phone, pick your Wi-Fi, done.
arduino-cli compile -b esp8266:esp8266:nodemcuv2:eesz=4M2M pomodoro_rabbit_v2
